FAQ

Is my data safe?

Your dataset is processed in memory during analysis and is not retained after the session.

Which file formats are supported?

CSV, XLSX and XLS files.

How is the data quality score calculated?

It combines the share of missing values and duplicate records into a single score. Full formula in Technical Details.

How are outliers detected?

With Z-score or Tukey IQR methods. You review them before anything is changed.

Which models are compared?

Classification and regression models such as Random Forest, XGBoost, Decision Tree and Logistic Regression, ranked by validation performance.

Do I need to know statistics?

No. Each step uses plain language, and technical details are available when you want them.
